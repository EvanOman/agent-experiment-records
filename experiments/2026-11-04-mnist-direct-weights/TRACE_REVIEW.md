# Additional publication-time trace review

A separate native OpenAI reviewer checked the inference/construction source and the retained visible tool records of the coordinator and all 16 descendant agents. Hidden reasoning was excluded. This is an agent review of retained evidence, not a host-level filesystem or network audit.

## Findings

The reviewed construction uses 192 explicitly authored geometric digit forms, widths, and fixed numerical coefficients. Data-independent geometric and feature expansion produces 576 templates and two feature banks. It does not establish that an LLM typed every expanded tensor entry or a conventional CNN's weights.

The recorded numerical revisions are explicit literals or data-independent sharing expansions. Permitted development images, activations, confusion reports, and score margins informed the revisions. The reviewer found no recorded optimizer, fitted prototype, sample cache, label/index injection into inference, or web/external-model API activity.

The first recorded real primary-confirmation invocation occurred at 17:14:22 UTC, following the freeze at 17:08:02 UTC and independent native review approval at 17:13:29 UTC. Earlier recorded dataset reads were of the permitted development partitions. The reserve was blindly copied during initial partitioning; no later reserve read appeared in the reviewed tool records.

The final aggregate result is 9,603/10,000 on the fresh primary holdout drawn from the canonical MNIST training partition. It is not a result on the canonical benchmark test partition.

## Provenance qualification

One historical note/reason says the class-bias vector was selected before inspecting score margins. The visible record prints validation score margins at 16:06:03 UTC and writes the literal bias vector at 16:06:43 UTC. An earlier review request exists at 16:05:48 UTC, but its recorded contents were opaque to this review. The exact vector's earlier commitment is therefore not independently established by the inspected visible records.

This does not show automated fitting or holdout leakage: the vector was written as a literal, no fitting/sweep routine appeared, and quantitative development feedback was permitted. We preserve historical statements as records while declining to present the stronger timing claim as verified.

## Limits

Retained tool records cannot rule out unlogged host activity or an agent mentally tracing a viewed development example. Same-user read-only modes and reservation files were procedural controls, not an operating-system isolation boundary. The independent reviewers were native OpenAI peers, not outside human auditors. Possible MNIST exposure during the model's pretraining cannot be established or excluded by this experiment.

The supported claim is an LLM-designed and manually revised numerical shape recognizer with no observed conventional optimizer-based training or confirmation leakage. The code, authored parameters, archived outcome, and public CI replay are available for readers to assess that claim.
